# REFERENCE SHEET — Thota Mitra System, Alerts, Spray Windows & Outbreak Reminders

Source: Thota Mitra (ತೋಟ ಮಿತ್ರ) Decision Support System, Frontiers in Plant Science monsoon epidemiology, Kerala Agricultural University (KAU) Package of Practices, and ICAR-Central Plantation Crops Research Institute (CPCRI).

## 1. THOTA MITRA SYSTEM, ALERTS AND NOTIFICATIONS (ಎಚ್ಚರಿಕೆ ಮತ್ತು ಸೂಚನೆಗಳು)
- **On-Dashboard Visual & Audio Alerts (ದೃಶ್ಯ ಮತ್ತು ಕನ್ನಡ ಧ್ವನಿ ಎಚ್ಚರಿಕೆ)**:
  Thota Mitra provides real-time on-dashboard visual badges and kn-IN audio voice speech synthesis alerts for spray windows. Whenever a dry window of 4 or more consecutive hours (rain <= 1.0 mm/hr, wind speed <= 15 km/h) is detected in the forecast, the dashboard displays a prominent green "ಸೂಕ್ತ / GOOD" alert banner and can read out the recommendation aloud in clear Kannada. If continuous rainfall or gusts exceeding 15 km/h are expected, it issues a red "ಬೇಡ / AVOID" alert to protect farmers from chemical wash-off and climber slip hazards.
- **KAU 40-Day Bordeaux Schedule Reminders (೪೦ ದಿನಗಳ ಬೋರ್ಡೋ ಸಿಂಪರಣೆ ಜ್ಞಾಪನೆ)**:
  In accordance with the Kerala Agricultural University (KAU) Package of Practices, Thota Mitra tracks a 3-round Bordeaux mixture schedule:
  - Round 1: Prophylactic 1% Bordeaux spray before South-West monsoon onset (late May / early June).
  - Round 2: Automated reminder around 40 days after the first application during a practical rain break (mid-July).
  - Round 3: Repeat reminder after a further 40 days (August/September) if monsoon rains remain active or extended.
- **40–50 Day Post-First-Rain Koleroga Outbreak Monitoring Alert (ಕೊಳೆರೋಗ ಉಲ್ಬಣ ಎಚ್ಚರಿಕೆ)**:
  Grounded in peer-reviewed literature (*Frontiers in Plant Science*) and *KAU Package of Practices*, fruit rot (Koleroga / Mahali caused by *Phytophthora meadii*) initiation in patchy-rain monsoon seasons commonly develops 40–50 days after the first monsoon rain. Thota Mitra generates an active outbreak monitoring alert at this 40–50 day mark, urging growers to inspect bunches closely and identify water-soaked lesions early.
- **Phase 2 Roadmap (SMS & WhatsApp Push Notifications)**:
  While Phase 1 provides instant on-dashboard visual notifications, timeline indicators, and kn-IN audio alerts, direct automated SMS and WhatsApp push notifications are actively scheduled as part of the Phase 2 roadmap.

## 2. HOW THE SPRAY WINDOW WORKS (ಸಿಂಪರಣೆ ಕಿಟಕಿ ಹೇಗೆ ಕಾರ್ಯನಿರ್ವಹಿಸುತ್ತದೆ?)
- **Adhesion Requirement**: Bordeaux mixture requires at least 3 to 4 hours of rain-free sunshine or dry weather to dry and adhere firmly to bunch stalks and nut surfaces.
- **Criteria for Spray Windows**:
  - **GOOD (ಸೂಕ್ತ)**: Forecast dry window >= 4 consecutive hours with rainfall <= 1.0 mm and wind speed <= 15 km/h. Fungicide adhesion is optimal.
  - **AVOID (ಬೇಡ)**: Continuous rain (> 1.0 mm) or high winds (> 15 km/h). Spraying is wasted due to wash-off and dangerous for crown climbing.
  - **MONITOR (ಗಮನಿಸಿ)**: Marginal intermittent conditions (1-3 hours dry). Watch morning sunshine and local rain radar before deploying labor.

## 3. CONDUCIVE CONDITIONS INDEX (ಹವಾಮಾನ ಅನುಕೂಲಕರ ಸೂಚ್ಯಂಕ)
- The Conducive Conditions Index (0 to 100) is a transparent heuristic indicator measuring atmospheric favorability for Koleroga (Phytophthora) fungal sporulation.
- **Driving Factors**:
  1. High relative humidity (> 85% - 90%)
  2. Cool monsoon temperatures (20°C to 24°C)
  3. Consecutive rainy days and heavy cloud cover
  4. Intermittent wet spells alternating with brief sun breaks
- **Status**: This index is an agronomic heuristic awaiting field validation. It indicates weather favorability, not a clinical disease probability or agricultural guarantee.

## 4. BORDEAUX MIXTURE PREPARATION & APPLICATION (ಬೋರ್ಡೋ ದ್ರಾವಣ ಮತ್ತು ಮದ್ದು ಸಿಂಪರಣೆ)
- **Standard 1% Formula**: Dissolve 1 kg of copper sulphate (ಮೈಲುತುತ್ತ) in 50 L water; in a separate bucket slake 1 kg of quicklime (ಸುಣ್ಣ) in 50 L water. Pour copper sulphate into lime suspension while stirring continuously. Check neutral pH with a clean blade or litmus.
- **Crop Protection**: Apply to arecanut bunches thoroughly before heavy rains. Maintain canopy drainage and remove fallen rot nuts.

## 5. PLANTATION ECONOMICS, MANDI PRICES & PROFIT (ವೆಚ್ಚ, ಬೆಲೆ ಮತ್ತು ಲಾಭದ ಲೆಕ್ಕಾಚಾರ)
- **Cost Structure**: Labor accounts for ~70-75% of total arecanut cultivation cost (climber wages, harvesting, processing).
- **Mandi Prices & Break-even**: APMC market prices (Kundapura, Mangalore, Sirsi, Shivamogga, Channagiri, etc.) are integrated to calculate break-even selling price per quintal and total net plantation profit.

## 6. KANNADA SUMMARY & FAQS (ಕನ್ನಡ ಸಾರಾಂಶ ಮತ್ತು ಪ್ರಶ್ನೋತ್ತರಗಳು)
- **ಎಚ್ಚರಿಕೆಗಳು (Alerts & Notifications)**: ತೋಟ ಮಿತ್ರವು ೪ ಗಂಟೆಗಳ ಒಣ ಹವೆ (ಮಳೆ <= 1.0 mm, ಗಾಳಿ <= 15 km/h) ಸಿಕ್ಕಾಗ ಡ್ಯಾಶ್‌ಬೋರ್ಡ್‌ನಲ್ಲಿ ಹಸಿರು "ಸೂಕ್ತ" ಎಚ್ಚರಿಕೆ ಮತ್ತು ಕನ್ನಡ ಆಡಿಯೋ (kn-IN) ಧ್ವನಿ ಸಂದೇಶ ನೀಡುತ್ತದೆ. KAU ೪೦ ದಿನಗಳ ಬೋರ್ಡೋ ಸಿಂಪರಣೆ ಜ್ಞಾಪನೆ ಹಾಗೂ ಮೊದಲ ಮಳೆಯ ೪೦-೫೦ ದಿನಗಳ ನಂತರದ ಕೊಳೆರೋಗ ಉಲ್ಬಣ ಎಚ್ಚರಿಕೆಯನ್ನು ನೀಡುತ್ತದೆ. SMS/WhatsApp ಸಂದೇಶಗಳು ಹಂತ-೨ ರಲ್ಲಿ ಲಭ್ಯವಾಗಲಿವೆ.
- **ಸಿಂಪರಣೆ ಕಿಟಕಿ (Spray Window)**: ಬೋರ್ಡೋ ದ್ರಾವಣ ಕಾಯಿಗಳಿಗೆ ಸರಿಯಾಗಿ ಅಂಟಿಕೊಳ್ಳಲು ಕನಿಷ್ಠ ೪ ಗಂಟೆಗಳ ಮಳೆರಹಿತ ಒಣ ವಾತಾವರಣ ಬೇಕು.
- **ಹವಾಮಾನ ಸೂಚ್ಯಂಕ (Conducive Conditions Index)**: ಹೆಚ್ಚು ತೇವಾಂಶ (>85%), ತಂಪಾದ ಹವೆ (20-24°C) ಮತ್ತು ನಿರಂತರ ಮಳೆಯಿಂದ ಕೊಳೆರೋಗ ಹರಡುವ ಸಾಧ್ಯತೆಯನ್ನು ೦-೧೦೦ ರ ಸೂಚ್ಯಂಕದಲ್ಲಿ ಅಳೆಯಲಾಗುತ್ತದೆ.
- **೧% ಬೋರ್ಡೋ ತಯಾರಿಕೆ (1% Bordeaux)**: ೧ ಕೆಜಿ ಮೈಲುತುತ್ತ + ೧ ಕೆಜಿ ಸುಣ್ಣ + ೧೦೦ ಲೀಟರ್ ನೀರು.
- **ವೆಚ್ಚ ಮತ್ತು ಲಾಭ (Cost & Profit)**: ಅಡಿಕೆ ಕೃಷಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಸುಮಾರು ಶೇ. ೭೦-೭೫ ರಷ್ಟು ಖರ್ಚು ಕೂಲಿಗೆ ತಗಲುತ್ತದೆ. ಮಂಡಿ ದರ ಮತ್ತು ಇಳುವರಿ ಆಧರಿಸಿ ಲಾಭವನ್ನು ಲೆಕ್ಕಹಾಕಲಾಗುತ್ತದೆ.
