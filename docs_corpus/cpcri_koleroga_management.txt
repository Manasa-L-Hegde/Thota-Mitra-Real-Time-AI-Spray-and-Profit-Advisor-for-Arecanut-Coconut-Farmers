REFERENCE SHEET — ICAR-CPCRI Koleroga / fruit-rot advisory
Source: ICAR–Central Plantation Crops Research Institute (CPCRI), plantation crops advisory material. This is a structured summary, not a verbatim PDF transcription. Check the current local CPCRI or Horticulture Department advisory before application.

SCOPE AND FIELD GUIDANCE
- Fruit rot (Koleroga/Mahali) can cause substantial monsoon-season loss; published reports cite a broad 10–75% range depending on site and season.
- CPCRI guidance describes low temperature, high humidity, and alternating rain and sunshine as favourable conditions. It does not provide the app's numerical weather thresholds.
- Maintain canopy hygiene and drainage; remove fallen diseased material and avoid standing water around palms.
- Apply 1% Bordeaux mixture to bunches as prophylaxis before sustained monsoon conditions, following local extension guidance.

1% BORDEAUX MIXTURE
- Core preparation reference: 1 kg copper sulphate plus 1 kg quicklime in 100 L water.
- A rosin/sticker addition is NOT part of the core 1% Bordeaux formula in this reference sheet. In high-rainfall areas it may be considered only after checking with local extension staff for the product label and local recommendation.
