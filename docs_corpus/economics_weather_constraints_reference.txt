REFERENCE SHEET — Deccan Herald and Krishikosh contextual evidence
Sources: Deccan Herald reporting on Sringeri/plantation rain-delay constraints; Krishikosh arecanut economics studies. Structured reference note; not a primary agronomy prescription.

WEATHER AND OPERATIONS
- A Sringeri field report described heavy June rain restricting timely spraying, with only about 40% of growers completing coverage during that period. This is contextual reporting, not a universal percentage.
- Continuous rain can prevent a practical dry adhesion window. Thota Mitra therefore identifies forecast dry windows; its numeric rain and wind cut-offs are heuristic and awaiting field validation.

ECONOMICS
- A Kasaragod benchmark reported labour at approximately 75% of total cultivation cost and production cost around ₹150/kg. Treat it as a regional benchmark, not a default for every plantation.
